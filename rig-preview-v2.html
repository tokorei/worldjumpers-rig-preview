About this version

Candidate B uses the original robot mesh and textures with a Mixamo skeleton. The model has 65 bones including finger chains, and at most four skin influences per vertex.

Start walking transitions into Walk. Stop walking transitions into Idle. The walking sample has a swagger; running uses the Jogging clip.

The blaster, flash and sound are review aids. The approved VR gun blue model (448 triangles) follows the right palm. The left arm uses a preview IK adjustment to place its support palm alongside the right-hand grip, retaining the animated finger curls. This adapts the existing Mixamo motion; dedicated pistol animation and contact clearance still need review. This preview fit still needs controller and VR IK validation. These animations have not been integrated into Unreal or tested in PICO.

The original gallery assets and V1 rig are preserved.
